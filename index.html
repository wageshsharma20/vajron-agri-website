Spraying and precision application

Dosage that follows ground speed

A digital flow sensor feeds the flight controller, which adjusts pump output automatically.

Spray pump

Brushless centrifugal, self-priming and IP67. Up to 8 L/min at 0.5 MPa, powered at 12S–14S (44–60.9 V DC) from the main flight battery.

Y-type nozzles

Four dual-outlet precision nozzles, eight spray points in total, positioned for uniform lateral coverage.

Digital flow sensor

Real-time flow monitoring feeds the precision-spraying function, which matches dosage to ground speed automatically.

Plumbing kit

A complete tank-to-pump-to-nozzle line with quick-connect fittings, rated for field pesticide and fertiliser use.
